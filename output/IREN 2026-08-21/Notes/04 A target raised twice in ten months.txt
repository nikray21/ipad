SLIDE 04 — A target raised twice in ten months   (28s)
ON SCREEN: The target is 30× the run-rate IREN has actually delivered.

• So what is actually moving this stock?
• Not that chart — this one.
• This is the number IREN talks about instead: its year-end AI Cloud run-rate target.
• November: three point four billion.
• February: unchanged.
• May: raised to three point seven.
• Three weeks ago: raised again, past four billion, on eighty-five percent of it under contract.
• Three upward revisions in ten months, each one its own press release, each one moving the stock — while actual reported AI Cloud revenue last quarter, annualized, comes to about a hundred thirty-four million.
• That target is roughly thirty times the run-rate the company has actually delivered so far.
