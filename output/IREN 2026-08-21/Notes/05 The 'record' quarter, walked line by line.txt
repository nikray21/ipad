SLIDE 05 — The 'record' quarter, walked line by line   (28s)
ON SCREEN: 173% of the 'record' profit is a paper gain.

• Finding two, and it explains the one quarter that looked great.
• Back in September, IREN reported net income of three hundred eighty-four million six — a record, and the headline everywhere.
• Walk it.
• Running the business that quarter actually lost seventy-six million four.
• Add back a six hundred sixty-five million dollar unrealized gain — a paper markup on derivatives tied to their convertible notes, nothing to do with operations — and the picture flips to five hundred seventy-five million of pretax income.
• Subtract tax, and you get your record quarter.
• Every dollar of this company's only profitable quarter came from an accounting mark, not from selling anything.
