SLIDE 08 — Cheap against miners, pricey against CoreWeave   (22s)   [CAN CUT]
ON SCREEN: IREN costs 0.6× its direct peers, 5.5× CoreWeave.

• So what are you paying for this?
• Same yardstick for everyone: market value against the latest reported quarter, annualized.
• Against its closest peers — the other Bitcoin miners pivoting to AI Cloud — IREN actually trades cheaper than Cipher Mining or TeraWulf.
• But against CoreWeave, the biggest pure AI Cloud comparison, IREN costs several times as much per dollar of sales, and CoreWeave is growing faster.
• Cheap for a miner.
• Not cheap for an AI Cloud company.
