SLIDE 03 — Revenue is down 40% since the peak   (28s)
ON SCREEN: AI Cloud's share went 4% to 23% as revenue shrank.

• Finding one.
• Here is the trap in this story.
• Each dot is a quarter — total revenue across the bottom, the AI Cloud share of that revenue up the side.
• Watch the path: revenue peaks in September at two hundred forty million, then falls two quarters running to a hundred forty-five million — down forty percent from the peak.
• And over that same stretch, AI Cloud's share of revenue climbs from three percent to twenty-three percent.
• Both things are true at once: the AI pivot is real, and Bitcoin-mining revenue is disappearing faster than AI Cloud can replace it.
• That is not a growth story.
• Not yet.
